​Technical Overview
​This specification details the structural design, articulation mechanics, and material integration for the M.A.D.wear™ Accordion Sleeve Extension Joint and Variable Cuff Expansion Panel.
​These modular components eliminate sizing friction across age groups, body types, and footwear/glove requirements—allowing a single base garment to adjust dynamically for limb length, joint articulation, and heavy boot or glove clearance.
​1. Accordion Sleeve Extension Joint (Elbow / Bicep Articulation)
​The sleeve joint uses a layered, multi-pleat stretch bellows structure that expands under axial tension without exposing skin or inner protective linings to environmental hazards.

Key Mechanical Specifications
​Expansion Range: 2.5 inches (63.5 mm) total linear extension per arm.
​Pleat Configuration: 4-fold concentric accordion bellows constructed from high-tenacity stretch fabric with an internal elastic memory rib.
​Flexure Dynamics: Flexes up to 135 degrees at the elbow joint without binding, pulling up the lower cuff, or placing strain on shoulder seams.
​Barrier Lining: Inner continuous hydrophobic gusset behind the pleats prevents water, hot embers, sparks, or debris from entering during maximum extension.
​2. Variable Cuff Expansion Panel (Boot & Ankle / Wrist Adaptor)
​The cuff panel utilizes a gusseted expansion slit secured by a continuous hook-and-loop strip and quick-cinch tension tab. This enables a single pant leg or sleeve cuff to fit over bulky turnout/welding boots or tighten securely around low-profile ankles and wrists.

Key Mechanical Specifications
​Expansion Ratio: Adjusts from a closed diameter of 8.0 inches (203 mm) up to an expanded flare of 14.5 inches (368 mm).
​Gusset Geometry: Sealed triangular V-gusset that tucks neatly flat when cinched, eliminating exterior fabric bunching.
​Fastening System: High-cycle hook-and-loop track paired with a heavy-duty pull tab for easy one-handed adjustment while wearing thick work gloves.
​Abrasion Guard: Bottom edge bound with high-denier Kevlar® webbing to prevent heel drag or cuff fraying on rough terrain or concrete shop floors.
